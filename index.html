CUSTOMER RESEARCH · ACADEMIC STUDY

Quick commerce in Patna

Is faster delivery worth charging for, or should platforms focus on making standard delivery more reliable?

Read the case study
Question
How are quick-commerce apps changing buying behavior in Patna, and is there demand for a faster-delivery fee?
Evidence
On the pricing question, 45.2% said no, 38.7% said maybe, and 16.1% said yes. These are stated preferences, not observed purchases.
Recommendation
Test clearer fees and more reliable standard delivery first. Run a small pricing test before treating faster delivery as a paid upgrade.

OPERATIONS ANALYTICS · JOB SIMULATION

Manufacturing downtime

A Tableau view to help operations teams decide where to investigate equipment downtime first.

Read the case study
Question
Where should an operations team start investigating equipment downtime?
Approach
In Deloitte’s Forage job simulation, I built a Tableau dashboard to compare downtime across factory locations and device categories.
Recommendation
Start with the factory and device category showing the most downtime. Check device counts and maintenance records before attributing a cause.

DATA VALIDATION · INTERNSHIP

Field verification and reporting

Making records from multiple districts easier to check, reconcile, and report.

Read the case study
Question
How do you make sure records from different districts are ready to use in a consolidated report?
Approach
At Rodic Consultants, I verified and analyzed project records across districts in Bihar and took on team coordination responsibilities.
Takeaway
Keep verified records separate from items still needing follow-up. Reporting totals without showing what remains unresolved can give a misleading picture.
