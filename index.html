CUSTOMER RESEARCH · ACADEMIC STUDY

Quick commerce in Patna

Is faster delivery worth charging for, or should platforms focus on making standard delivery more reliable?

Read the case study
Question
How are quick-commerce apps changing buying behavior in Patna, and is there demand for a faster-delivery fee?
Evidence
On the pricing question, 45.2% said no, 38.7% said maybe, and 16.1% said yes. These are stated preferences, not observed purchases.
Recommendation
Test clearer fees and more reliable standard delivery first. Run a small pricing test before treating faster delivery as a paid upgrade.

REQUIREMENTS & RESEARCH · ASSIGNMENT

Screening companies before scoring

How I caught a promising-looking manufacturer that didn’t meet the shortlist criteria.

Read the case study
Question
How do you keep an ineligible company out of a shortlist when it looks promising at first glance?
Approach
I used AI to surface leads, then manually checked revenue and ownership against the screening rules. One candidate failed both checks, so I documented why and excluded it before scoring.
What changed
I moved revenue and ownership checks to the front of the workflow so each decision could be traced back to the evidence.

FINANCE ANALYTICS · EXPLORATORY PROJECT

What bank filings are saying

Comparing two ways to measure tone in SEC filings and see how it lines up with market data.

Read the case study
Question
Do a finance-trained language model and a financial dictionary tell the same story about management’s tone?
Approach
I built a Python pipeline that extracts management commentary from SEC filings for five banks, scores it with FinBERT and a financial dictionary, and aligns the results with market moves and earnings surprises.
Takeaway
Compare the methods side by side before drawing conclusions. This is an exploratory analysis, not a forecast or a trading signal.

FINANCIAL ANALYSIS · TABLEAU PROJECT

When profit and cash tell different stories

A dashboard examining a decade of financial statements for Apollo Micro Systems.

Read the case study
Question
Did reported profit growth translate into operating cash flow?
Approach
I built a Tableau dashboard covering FY2016–FY2025 to compare profit after tax with operating cash flow and examine working-capital and share-dilution trends.
Takeaway
When profit and cash diverge, check which working-capital accounts moved before concluding that earnings quality improved or deteriorated.
